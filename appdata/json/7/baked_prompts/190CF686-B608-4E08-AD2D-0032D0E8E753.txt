# Baked Prompt 1

 SUBJECT:
  Contaminated tilberi creation on floor - industrial rope skeleton visible
  through translucent grey-white flesh forming from wool and blood, brass
  button functioning as eye with green iris weeping verdigris tears,
  breathing in three simultaneous rhythms (8/min human, 12/min house, 20/min
   sheep).

  ACTION:
  The wrong thing moves. Rope bones visible through forming flesh. Brass
  button-eye weeps green tears burning floor. Breathes in three different
  rhythms simultaneously, each from different body part creating undulation.
   Tries standing but rope-bones bend wrong way, falls, tries again.
  Movement sounds like rope creaking mixed with newborn cry. Sigrid reaches
  toward it - maternal instinct despite horror. It reaches back with
  appendage part hoof, part hand, part mechanical grabber. Size of cat but
  growing, feeding on industrial contamination. Through windows all four
  landvættir watching - trawler lights, steam, house breathing, raven
  shadow.

  SCENE:
  Turf house interior with biological consciousness - driftwood beams flex
  like ribs, turf walls show blood vessel patterns, floor warm spots glow
  with organ function, lamp flame syncs with house breathing. Floor with
  abomination, family frozen in horror. Temperature -23°C.

  STYLE:
  Close on creature revealing impossible anatomy, pulling back to show
  witnesses (that's where the camera is), documentary of wrong birth.

  ASPECT: 4:3

  META: Iceland, Westfjords 1888
