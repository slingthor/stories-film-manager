# Baked Prompt 1

Men in westfjords, Iceland, on the sea in arabatur wooden boats. They are pursuing a whale on the boat. You can see the men pursuing it with the spears, and they throw 3 spears at the whale. It is an icelandic whale. Its a gloomy mid day and there is no sun. The whale becomes very injured, but does not die. The men are dressed in traditional icelandic 1880 clothing for winter and sea.
